=== regression fixed on freeze day ===
  Day 6 rate limiting silently broke every route without an explicit
  RateLimitOptions block: /api/rates, /api/auth/login and /api/checkout
  all returned 503. With a global ClientIdHeader set, Ocelot applies a
  default empty rule to any route that does not opt out, then cannot
  identify the client. Every route now states its position explicitly.
